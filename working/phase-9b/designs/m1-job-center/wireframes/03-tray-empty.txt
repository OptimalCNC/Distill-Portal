Notes
-----
- A single muted italic line. No sectioned skeleton ("Active (0)" /
  "Recent (0)") — those subdivisions are noisy when the tray has
  nothing to show.
- Production: when EITHER section has zero entries while the other has
  ≥1, the populated section renders its label + cards and the empty
  section renders a smaller "No active operations." / "No recent
  operations." italic line beneath its own label (see 01- and 04-
  wireframes). The single "No operations." copy is reserved for the
  truly-empty tray.
