Notes
-----
- Tray surface is opaque --color-surface; 1px border-inline-start
  --color-border separates it from the dimmed page beneath the scrim.
- The horizontal divider between sections is 1px --color-border with
  --space-6 above and --space-4 below the section header.
- Each op card is a native <details>; the summary row is the always-
  visible block above. The dashed mid-rule inside the card separates
  the kind/status row from the action/summary row.
- Pills sit at the right edge of the top row; cancel buttons sit at
  the right edge of the bottom row. The grid keeps them aligned
  vertically.
