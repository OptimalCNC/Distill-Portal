Phase 9b — ActionBar trigger button (replaces 9a's .action-bar-operation-badge)
================================================================================

The 9a `.action-bar-operation-pill` (last-completed) is REMOVED in 9b. Its
information now lives in the tray's Recent section. The 9a badge becomes a
labelled button with an inline count chip.

count = 0  (no non-terminal operations)
                                              hairline-bordered chip is HIDDEN
+-----------------------------------------+   when count=0; the button label
| Rescan  | last rescan 3 m ago | Import   |  itself stays visible.
|         selected (0) | [ Job Center  ]   |
+-----------------------------------------+


count = 1
+-----------------------------------------+   small chip "1" in accent-mix
| Rescan  | ... | [ Job Center  [ 1 ] ]    |  fill, mono tabular numerals.
+-----------------------------------------+


count = 2
+-----------------------------------------+
| Rescan  | ... | [ Job Center  [ 2 ] ]    |
+-----------------------------------------+


count > 9
+-----------------------------------------+   chip shows "9+" (aria-label
| Rescan  | ... | [ Job Center  [ 9+ ] ]   |  reads the actual integer).
+-----------------------------------------+


Anatomy of the trigger button
-----------------------------

    [ Job Center  [ N ] ]
    ^             ^
    |             +-- inline count chip
    |                 - 1px hairline border at accent 35% / surface mix
    |                 - background at accent 10% / surface mix
    |                 - text at accent 75% / ink mix
    |                 - font-mono, --text-xs, tabular numerals
    |                 - min-width 1.5rem so single-digit + "9+" share width
    |                 - HIDDEN when count = 0
    +-- button shell
        - 1px solid --color-border-strong border
        - --color-surface background, --color-text label
        - font-chrome, --text-base
        - aria-haspopup="dialog", aria-controls="jc-dialog"
        - aria-expanded toggles with dialog open/close
        - focus ring: 2px solid --color-accent at outline-offset 2px
        - hover lift: border-color → --color-text
