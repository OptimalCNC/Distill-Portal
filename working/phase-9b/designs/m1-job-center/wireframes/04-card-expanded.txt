Card anatomy + behavior
-----------------------

- The card is a native <details>. Browser handles toggle + ARIA.
- The visible <summary> contains the grid (icon | label/time | pill) +
  bottom row (cancel|summary). The native marker is hidden via
  list-style: none + ::-webkit-details-marker: none.
- The expanded subtree appends a <dl> with submitted/finished
  timestamps + a <pre> with pretty JSON.
- Phase 8 upgrade path: when the bespoke JSON inspector lands (Phase 8
  M2/M3), the <pre> here is swapped for the inspector component
  without touching the <details>/<summary> shell. The data flow is
  unchanged: `operation.result_json` / `operation.error_json` is the
  single source.
- Active ops (queued / running / cancel_requested) DO NOT show the
  expand area — there's no terminal result_json yet. The <details>
  remains usable; expanding it just reveals an empty section showing
  Submitted timestamp only.
