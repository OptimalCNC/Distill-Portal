Phase 9b — Status pill catalog (7 variants)
============================================

All seven status pills sit at --text-xs, font-chrome, uppercase,
letter-spacing 0.04em, --radius-sm. Each carries a 0.5rem-square dot at
the left edge that doubles as a non-color disambiguator. Border style
(solid / dotted / dashed) is the second disambiguator.

         pill            border       dot                  recipe
        ------         --------     ------       --------------------------
QUEUED  [ • queued ]   1px dotted   hollow ring  fill: surface
                       border-stg                text: ink-muted
                                                 border: border-strong dotted

RUNNING [● running ]   1px solid    filled dot,  fill: accent 10% / surface
                       accent-mix   pulsing      text: accent 75% / ink
                                                 border: accent 35% / surface

CANCEL_ [░ cancel_req.] 1px dashed  filled dot   fill: warn 12% / surface
REQ.                    warn-mix                 text: warn 75% / ink
                                                 border: warn 55% / surface
                                                         (DASHED)

SUCC.   [● succeeded ] 1px solid    filled dot   fill: success 12% / surface
                       success-mix                text: success 75% / ink
                                                 border: success 35% / surface

FAILED  [● failed ]    1px solid    filled dot   fill: error 12% / surface
                       error-mix                  text: error 75% / ink
                                                 border: error 35% / surface

CANC.   [■ cancelled]  1px solid    filled        fill: surface-raised
                       border-stg   SQUARE dot   text: ink-muted
                                                 border: border-strong

INTER.  [□ interr.]    1px dashed   hollow        fill: surface
                       border-stg   SQUARE       text: ink-muted
                                                 border: border-strong DASHED

Visual disambiguation matrix
----------------------------

Property        queued   running   cancel_req   succeeded   failed   cancelled   interrupted
border style    dotted   solid     dashed       solid       solid    solid       dashed
border tint     neutral  accent    warn         success     error    neutral     neutral
fill tint       none     accent    warn         success     error    neutral     none
text tint       muted    accent    warn         success     error    muted       muted
dot shape       round    round     round        round       round    square      square
dot fill        hollow   filled    filled       filled      filled   filled      hollow
animation       —        pulsing   —            —           —        —           —

Result: every pair of statuses differs in at least TWO of {border-style,
fill-tint, text-tint, dot-shape, dot-fill, animation} — robust to
monochromatic vision, monochrome printing, and brightness-only displays.
