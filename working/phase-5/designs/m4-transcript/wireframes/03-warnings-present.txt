Notes
-----
- Banner is a native <details> element. Expand/collapse uses the M2b
  block-size animation exemption (200 ms ease-in-out).
- List rendering: `line {N} · {reason}` per warning, in --font-mono
  --text-xs --color-ink-muted.
- Banner background is --color-surface-raised (NOT warn-tinted). Warnings
  are diagnostic, not capacity. The raised surface marks them as machinery.
- Dismiss button: hairline border, 4 px radius, focus-visible at accent.
- ARIA: <details> is browser-native; Enter/Space toggles via the <summary>;
  arrow keys navigate naturally.
