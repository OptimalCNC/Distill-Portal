Notes
-----
- The scoped TranscriptView is REUSED VERBATIM from M4. The new
  optional `messageRange` prop slices `parsed.messages` to the agent
  reaction range `[block.start+1, block.end]` (inclusive both ends,
  per planner Q10).

- Per-message rendering inherits M4's recipe entirely:
  - `msg-assistant`: bare surface panel with attribution row.
  - `msg-tool-use`: hairline-bordered chrome with disclosure.
  - `msg-tool-result`: hairline-bordered chrome + collapsible tail.
  - All messages respect the 70ch reading measure within the
    `transcript-stream`.

- a11y: Nested <section aria-label="..."> landmarks. The outer
  SkimView is `aria-label="Session skim outline"`; the inner scoped
  TranscriptView is `aria-label="Session transcript"`. Both are
  reachable via `getByRole("region", { name: ... })`.

- The scoped TranscriptView's stream container has its own
  rhythm rules (M4's three-magnitude rule for messages). NO override
  needed in M5; the messages render with M4 spacing.

- Performance: a 4-message scoped TranscriptView mounts trivially
  (well under 16ms). For larger reactions (e.g., 50+ messages),
  consider the long-corpus observation step in design.md §13.3.
